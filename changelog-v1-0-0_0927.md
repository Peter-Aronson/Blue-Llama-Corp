## Documentation for 9/27/2026:
- Today's session brought forth the creation of Project Blue Llama and its Organization for my project portfolio. After careful research into what a typical organization looks like, I created and mapped out 9 different departments, 39
  individual roles, 30 unique function groups, and 62 total seats within this Organization. As time goes on, I will also create a situation where a user will need deactivated due to offboarding, and document the process. Additionally, I will also
  create some dummy accounts that have been already deactivated to simulate employees who have departed the company. Near the end of the project will show a live demo of Joiner/Mover/Leaver. Each user will be carefully crafted and provisioned appropriately to their specific role.
  Screenshots, gifs, and other visual documentation with explanations for each decision will be included from here on out.


## What I learned:
- In today's session, I learned the process of planning a project using the Waterfall Methodology. I also learned the realistic hierarchy of a small organization, spanning across multiple departments with groups to enforce RBAC and Least Privilege.
  Additionally, after reviewing many examples of real-world organizations who follow similar structures, I created Blue Llama Group's department infrastructure alongside its individual groups, which only certain employees
  within each department will have access to respective and appropriate duties. Each department will have a group which is highlighted in purple, and those groups will have the power to approve or signoff on the subordinates' duties. This will be the visual plan documentation to build off
  of when it is time to begin the design and implementation phases. I also learned and created a diagram for cross-department handoffs, where each department will assist eachother with necessary duties and pass off certain duties to the appropriate team, again enforcing Least Privilege and RBAC.
